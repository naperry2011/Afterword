Afterword for iPhone

Afterword is made by one person. Email nuperry2011@gmail.com and you will get a reply from that person, usually within a day or two.

Common questions

Can't find a book in search. Open Library covers most books but not all. Tap "Add it by hand instead" and type the title and author yourself. It goes on the shelf like any other book.

Search says it didn't work. Search needs an internet connection. Everything else in the app works offline. You can always add the book by hand and come back later.

Moving to a new phone. Afterword has no account or sync. In Settings, tap Export as JSON and save the file somewhere you can reach from the new phone. Install Afterword there and use Import from JSON.

Where are my reflections? Open the book from the shelf. Your answers are listed under Reflections, and "Send a card" makes the recommendation card from your answer to "Who should read this?".

I marked a book finished by accident. Open the book and tap Reading again. Your answers, if any, are kept.

Something broken?

Email with what you tapped and what you expected to happen. Screenshots help. There are no crash reports or analytics, so the only way I hear about a problem is from you.
