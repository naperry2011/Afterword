Privacy

Afterword for iPhone · effective 17 September 2026

Afterword is a private reading journal. It has no account, no server, and no analytics. Everything you write stays on your phone.

What the app stores

Books you add, your reading status and progress, and your reflections are stored in a database on your device only. We cannot see them, and there is no way for us to retrieve them.

What leaves your device

  • Book search. When you search for a book, the title or author you type is sent to Open Library, a non-profit service, to find matching books and covers. No identifier for you or your device is included. Open Library's own privacy policy applies to that request.
  • Cards and exports you send. When you share a recommendation card or export your data, the file goes wherever you choose to send it, through the iOS share sheet. Afterword never sends anything on its own.

What we do not do

  • No accounts, sign-in, or sync.
  • No analytics, crash reporting, advertising, or tracking of any kind.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, location, or microphone.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app deletes everything it stored. To keep a copy first, use Export in Settings.

Children

Afterword does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
